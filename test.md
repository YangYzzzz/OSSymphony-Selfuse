# Add, Insert, Delete, Copy, Move, Rename a Worksheet in LibreOffice Calc
November 2, 2022 
by [![](https://secure.gravatar.com/avatar/23b5631159cc3e1b99859b8d130f3604e84b88b0b0152cf417b1a37f7e0c5673?s=80&d=mm&r=g)arindam](https://www.libreofficehelp.com/author/arindam/)

**Learn how to do basic LibreOffice Calc spreadsheet operations such as Add, Insert, Delete, etc.**

A new workbook contains only one Sheet named “Sheet1” in Calc by default.
To add a worksheet, Click on the small “+” sign at the bottom-left beside the first sheet. See below image.
[![add-sheet-sign](https://libreofficehelp.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/add-sheet-sign.png)](https://libreofficehelp.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/add-sheet-sign.png)
After you click that, Sheet2 will be added.
Right-click on the Sheet name (e.g. Sheet1, Sheet2 etc.) and choose the option “Insert Sheet”. A new dialog would be opened, which contains various options for inserting a sheet.
[![insert-sheet-menu](https://libreofficehelp.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/insert-sheet-menu.png)](https://libreofficehelp.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/insert-sheet-menu.png)
![insert-sheet](https://libreofficehelp.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/insert-sheet.png)
**Position:**
  * Choose “Before Current Sheet” if you want the new sheet to be inserted before the Sheet where you right-clicked.
  * Choose “After Current Sheet” if you want the new sheet to be inserted after the Sheet where you right-clicked.


**Sheet:**
  * If you want to insert only one Sheet, provide “No of sheets:” = 1 and choose a name.
  * If you want to insert multiple sheets, provide “No of sheets:” = any number you want. Those many number of sheets would be inserted with names in numeric order – sheet3, sheet4, sheet5.


To delete a sheet, right-click on the sheet which you want to delete. In the context menu, choose “Delete Sheet”.
[![delete-sheet](https://libreofficehelp.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/delete-sheet.png)](https://libreofficehelp.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/delete-sheet.png)
Choose Yes in the confirmation window.
[![delete-sheet-confirmation](https://libreofficehelp.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/delete-sheet-confirmation.png)](https://libreofficehelp.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/delete-sheet-confirmation.png)
To move or copy a sheet before or after an existing sheet OR move a sheet at the end of all the sheets, choose the option ‘Move/Copy Sheets’ by right-clicking on the sheet which you want to move/copy.
[![move-copy-sheet](https://libreofficehelp.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/move-copy-sheet.png)](https://libreofficehelp.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/move-copy-sheet.png)
To move: Choose the option “Move” and choose where you want to move from the “Insert before” sheet list.
[![move-sheet](https://libreofficehelp.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/move-sheet.png)](https://libreofficehelp.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/move-sheet.png)
To copy: Choose the option “Copy” and choose where you want to move from the “Insert before” sheet list. While copying, you can also give a new name to the copied sheet from the “New Name” text box.
[![copy-sheet](https://libreofficehelp.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/copy-sheet.png)](https://libreofficehelp.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/copy-sheet.png)
Right-click on the sheet which you want to rename and choose the option “Rename Sheet”.
[![rename-sheet](https://libreofficehelp.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/rename-sheet.png)](https://libreofficehelp.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/rename-sheet.png)
A new pop-up will be open; give the new name and press OK.
[![rename-sheet-new-name](https://libreofficehelp.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/rename-sheet-new-name.png)](https://libreofficehelp.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/rename-sheet-new-name.png)
